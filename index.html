AI news, checked against the source and tested by hand

AI Jissoku-shitsu is a Japanese-language channel on YouTube: youtube.com/@ai-jissoku. Each episode explains one new AI product or announcement, cites the primary source (official blog, release notes, documentation) in the description, and where possible shows our own measured test results. Every video has Japanese captions.

AI Jissoku Uploader

AI Jissoku Uploader is a private tool used only by the channel owner to publish the channel's own videos through the YouTube Data API: it uploads the finished video, its captions and thumbnail, edits the title/description when a correction is needed, posts the channel's own pinned comments and replies on its own videos, and reads the channel's own analytics. It is not offered to other users and does not read, collect or display any other person's data or any third-party YouTube content.

All videos are created by the channel owner. The owner decides every topic and personally checks every episode before it is made public: fact check against the primary source, script, pronunciation, captions and thumbnail. Topics that cannot be verified are dropped.
